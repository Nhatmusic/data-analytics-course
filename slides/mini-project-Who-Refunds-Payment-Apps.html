LEVEL 3 · THE DATASET

CFPB Consumer Complaint Database

Run by the U.S. Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. Real complaints from real people, updated every day, free and public.

🔗 consumerfinance.gov/data-research/consumer-complaints/search

  1. Open the link and set Date received: 2025-01-20 to 2026-11-08
  2. Under Product, tick Money transfer, virtual currency, or money service, then sub-product Mobile or digital wallet
  3. Click Export data → CSV → full results
  4. Open the file in Excel or Google Sheets and check the row count

This range keeps the file under 100k rows, small enough to download. The newest complaints may still be "In progress": watch for them.

🔍 Before you trust a result, run these 4 checks

1 · Guess first

Before running, write your guess. Way off? Find out why. Example: "I think about 20% got money back."

2 · Add it up

Group counts should add up to your total row count. Percentages stay between 0 and 100.

3 · Spot check

Pick one company. Filter it in Excel or Sheets. Same count as SQL? If not, one of them is wrong.

4 · Ask why

Ask Claude to explain your query line by line. Then change one line and predict the new result.

📸 In each weekly report, show one query + the check you ran on it.
BOSS LEVEL · ⚔️ CHALLENGES AI CAN'T SOLVE ALONE

Only your data knows the answer

Claude has never seen your file. It can suggest, but it can't know these answers. You have to run, check, and defend them. Do Boss 1 and Boss 2, then pick at least one more.

🕵️

Boss 1 · The Venmo hunt

Venmo has no row of its own. How many Venmo complaints are there? Your number depends on how you search. Defend your method.

🐛

Boss 2 · Catch AI wrong

Find at least one query where Claude's suggestion gave a wrong or misleading result on your data. Show the bad result, how you caught it, and the fix.

🔗

Boss 3 · Build a lookup table

Make your own small table that maps each company name to an app. Connect it to the complaints table instead of typing an App column by hand.

⏳

Boss 4 · Better or worse?

Did refunds get better or worse over time? First, make SQL understand the dates in this file. They may not look the way you expect.

⚡

Boss 5 · Who answers fastest?

How many days pass between a complaint and the company getting it? Which app is slowest? Watch for odd values.

⚖️

Boss 6 · Myth or fact?

Prove or bust: "Scam victims almost never get money back." Find the numbers, then say how sure you are and why.

🏆 In the final meeting, I compare your Boss 1 number with the others. If they differ, be ready to defend yours.

🕵️ Traps to watch for (click for a hint)

Company A has the most complaints. Is it the worst?

Hint: big companies have more customers. Compare rates (share closed with relief, share on time), not raw counts. Skip companies with fewer than 100 complaints.

Some rows say "In progress". Keep them?

Hint: the company hasn't answered yet. Can you count them as "no relief"?

One company, three spellings?

Hint: apps often appear under a parent company name. Look up who owns your app.

Does this data tell us which company is best?

Hint: who files complaints? Happy customers don't. And who reports the "relief"? The company itself.
