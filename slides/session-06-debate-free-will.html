The Echo Rule 🔁

One rule for the whole debate. Everything else is up to you.

The rule

Before you answer anyone, say their argument back. They confirm you got it right. Only then do you respond.
No echo, no rebuttal.

Say it like this: "What I heard you say is..."
You decide

Which points to make, which evidence to use, and how to argue back.

The debate ⚖️

Six steps. Tap a row for details. Every opening gets echoed right away.

The topic

"Resolved: Human free will is primarily an illusion created after our unconscious brain has already acted."

In the rebuttal

Every reply starts with the echo: summarize, confirm, then oppose with evidence. If your opponent says your summary is off, fix it before you continue.

Attack the reasoning, not the person
🔗 Correlation vs. causation: did X cause Y, or did they just happen together?
🎯 Assumptions: what must be true for their argument to work?
📖 Definitions: what does "free" mean here?
Example definition challenge

"Is an unconscious choice still 'free' if it comes from your own memories?"
